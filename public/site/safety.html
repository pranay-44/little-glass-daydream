Road safety & community voice

Data is only useful if it changes behaviour. Here are the safety habits our survey and traffic findings point to — short enough to read at a bus stop.

For pedestrians

🚸

Cross at the zebra

Use the zebra crossing or footbridge even when it feels far. Our survey shows jaywalking near the signal is the top risk.

⏰

Avoid the 5–7 PM rush

Vehicle counts peak at 6 PM. If you can, walk or shop before 5 PM or after 7:30 PM.

📵

Phone down, eyes up

Never cross while looking at a screen. Remove earphones before stepping onto the road.

For two-wheeler riders

🪖

Helmet, every ride

Strap an ISI-marked helmet properly — even for a two-minute ride to the station.

🛵

No triple riding

Three on a two-wheeler removes the rider's control exactly when the road is most crowded.

💡

Be seen at night

Keep headlights on and wear something reflective. Most evening accidents happen at low visibility.

What our community told us

Collected Data

In our mini survey (20 responses), 55% said they rarely feel safe crossing their main junction during peak hours, and 30% said only sometimes. Speeding vehicles and missing pedestrian signals were the most common reasons cited.

Our commitment

We will share these findings with our college's NSS wing and the local ward office, and re-run the count next semester to check whether peak-hour behaviour improved. Community engagement doesn't end with a website.
