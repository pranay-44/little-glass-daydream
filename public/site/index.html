Seeing traffic the way AI sees it

The problem

Peak-hour congestion at our neighbourhood junction grows every year, yet most commuters have no idea when the road is safest to use. Traffic data usually lives inside government systems — not in the hands of the people who cross that junction every day.

Our mission

Build a lightweight, mobile-first website that explains how AI counts vehicles, shares what we actually measured on the ground, and turns it into clear safety guidance — with every number honestly labelled by its source.
