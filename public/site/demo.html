This widget runs fully in your browser (Vanilla JavaScript). Thresholds: under 250 vehicles = smooth, 250–500 = moderate, above 500 = heavy congestion.

🟢 Smooth flow — under 250

The junction has spare capacity. A good window for new riders and pedestrians to travel or cross.

🟡 Moderate — 250 to 500

Traffic is building. Expect queueing at signals and allow extra time before crossing.

🔴 Heavy congestion — above 500

Matches our real 5–6 PM field readings. Avoid non-essential trips through the junction at this hour.

Why thresholds matter

This is the same logic an AI traffic system uses: detection gives a count, and a threshold turns the count into an actionable alert.
